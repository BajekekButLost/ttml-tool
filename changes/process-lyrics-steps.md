Choose what Process lyrics does, and run it safely more than once

Every step of **Process lyrics** (em dashes, splitting hyphenated words, parentheses to background lines and the rest) now has its own switch under **Advanced**. Running it twice no longer mangles the text, quotes and closing punctuation like `sorry,"` stay on their word, and tool changes in the import text can be undone with `Ctrl+Z`.
