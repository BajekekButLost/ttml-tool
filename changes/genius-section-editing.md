Editing lines inside Genius sections keeps the section together

A line you insert, paste, duplicate or drag into the middle of a section now joins that section, instead of splitting it into extra headers. Deleting that line again leaves the section whole. **Insert new line before/after** and **Move words to new line** keep the line's section too. Section headers update right away instead of after switching tabs. **Snap to playhead** no longer gives untimed words a time, and **Copy previous timing** finds earlier sections that start on an untimed line and says so when no lines match.
