Sync background lines in a separate pass

**Time › Background lines** (also in **Preferences › Editor & sync**) now has three modes. **Inline** works as before. **Separate pass** keeps **G** and **H** on main lines, so finishing a line jumps to the next main line instead of its background line; click a background line to sync the background lines on their own. **Skip** replaces the old "Skip background lines" checkbox.
