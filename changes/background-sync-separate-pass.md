Sync background lines in a separate pass

**Time › Background lines** (also in **Preferences › Editor & sync**) replaces the "Skip background lines" checkbox. **Inline** works as before. **Separate pass** keeps **G** and **H** on whichever kind of line you start on: start on a main line and they skip background lines, so finishing a line jumps to the next main line; click a background line and they move between background lines only.
