Sync background lines in a separate pass, or only background lines

**Time › Background lines** (also in **Preferences › Editor & sync**) now has four modes. **Inline** works as before. **Separate pass** keeps **G** and **H** on main lines, so finishing a line jumps to the next main line instead of its background line; click a background line to sync the background lines on their own. **Only** skips main lines so G and H move between background lines only. **Skip** replaces the old "Skip background lines" checkbox.
