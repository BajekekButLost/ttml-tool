The beginner guide, project folders and the new Preferences pages are translated

About 170 pieces of text showed in English (or Chinese) in every language: the whole beginner guide, folder and linked project messages, the Editor & Sync and Files & Storage pages in Preferences, Discord Rich Presence settings, window buttons, the font picker and several appearance options.
