Spicy preview now matches Spicy Lyrics for background vocals

A line now stays active until its **background vocal** ends, so the next line and interludes no longer start early. Background vocals placed before their line, consecutive and standalone background vocals, and split or hyphenated words render the way Spicy Lyrics renders them. Lines that aren't being sung are no longer redrawn every frame.
