Duplicate at playhead and auto-segment words from the right-click menu

The line menu has **Duplicate at playhead** (or **Duplicate into spectrogram selection** when one is active), showing its shortcut. **Ctrl+L** now works with any line selection, not only after **Duplicate to…**. The word menu in Edit mode has **Auto-segment selected words**, which splits just those words with your saved engine and Learned Splits.
