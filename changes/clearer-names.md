Clearer names for sync tools and settings

Names that people found confusing now say what they do. The F, G and H actions are **Start**, **Next** and **End**. **Time Offset** and **Commit Offset** are now **Sync offset** and **Next key offset**, **Sync Level** is **Sync by: Word / Line**, and **Sync to Spectrogram Cursor** is **Sync at mouse position**. **Sync Line Timestamps** is now **Fit lines to their words**, **Ignore Sync** is **Skip when syncing**, and **V-Sync** is **Smooth preview (every frame)**. Shortcuts, layouts and metadata fields use one name each, and the interface now uses sentence case throughout.
