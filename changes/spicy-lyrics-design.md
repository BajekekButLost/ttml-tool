New Spicy Lyrics design, on by default

The editor now uses the dark lavender look and Geist type from the rest of Spicy Lyrics. Turn it off in **Preferences → Appearance** to get the previous theme back. Preferences tabs fade in when clicked, toasts slide in without bouncing, and dialogs still fade with reduced motion on. The **Advanced** switch on the ribbons also stops showing as on when it's off.
