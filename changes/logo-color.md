Pick a color for the app icon

**Logo color** in Preferences › Appearance recolors the app icon: the window and taskbar icon, and on Windows the desktop and Start menu shortcuts, which keep the color after updates. In the browser it tints the logo in the title bar. Pick a preset, including the red and purple of earlier logos, or any custom color. It's part of appearance presets, Reset theme and settings backups.
