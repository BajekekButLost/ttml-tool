Tooltips on ribbon and mode buttons

Hover any ribbon control or the **Edit**, **Time** and **Preview** buttons to see what it does and its shortcut, which follows your own keybindings. The **Preview panel** toggle now sits in the Time ribbon without turning on Advanced. The **Background lines** options are now called **Sync with main** and **Skip while syncing** (previously Inline and Separate pass); your saved choice carries over.
