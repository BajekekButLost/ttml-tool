Spicy preview shows timing mistakes and what's left to sync

The Spicy preview marks words it would hide or play wrong in red with a dashed outline: a word with no timing in a timed line, one that ends before it starts, and one that starts before its line or ends after it. A split word gets one box, with the broken syllable in red. A count above the preview selects the first one in Time mode.

Lines you haven't synced yet now stay in the preview where they belong, drawn as hollow letters, instead of disappearing. A line you're in the middle of syncing shows right away, with the words you haven't reached still hollow, so you no longer have to finish the line to see it.
